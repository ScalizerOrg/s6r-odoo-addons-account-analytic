# Scalizer Purchase Stock Analytic Procurement

Propagate the analytic distribution to the purchase order lines generated by replenishment.

## Features

- The purchase order lines generated by the replenishment of an MTO product (without purchase request) take the
  analytic distribution of the need (manufacturing order, stock move).
- The needs having a different analytic distribution are not merged into the same purchase order line.
- The needs without analytic distribution keep their standard behavior.

## Usage

1. Set an analytic distribution on a manufacturing order whose components are supplied on order (MTO).
2. Confirm the manufacturing order: the generated request for quotation lines carry the same analytic distribution.
